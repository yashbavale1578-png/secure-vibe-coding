# Privacy, legal readiness, and data governance

This reference is a product and engineering checklist, not legal advice. Requirements depend on jurisdiction, sector, user location, data categories, business model, age of users, contracts, and processing roles.

## Data inventory and minimization
- Map data collected, source, purpose, lawful basis/authority where applicable, recipients, storage location, retention, and deletion path.
- Collect only what is necessary for stated purposes.
- Set retention schedules and deletion workflows.
- Provide data access, correction, portability, or deletion workflows where required.
- Protect children's data and obtain verifiable parental consent where legally required.
- Review cross-border transfers and subprocessors.

## Consent and tracking
- Inventory cookies, pixels, SDKs, analytics, embeds, and local storage.
- Determine which technologies require consent in each relevant jurisdiction.
- Block non-essential tracking until consent where required.
- Keep marketing consent separate from necessary service processing where required.
- Record consent version, time, purpose, and withdrawal where appropriate.
- Provide unsubscribe mechanisms for marketing messages.
- Do not use deceptive consent, dark patterns, or preselected optional consent.

## Policies and disclosures
Potentially applicable documents include privacy notice, terms of service, cookie notice, refund/cancellation policy, subscription/renewal disclosure, contact details, and security disclosure policy.
- Policies must match actual product behavior and vendors.
- Do not invent legal entities, addresses, certifications, uptime guarantees, or refund promises.
- DMCA registration is not universally required; assess applicability rather than adding a claim.
- Avoid false SOC 2 or other certification claims.
- State uptime commitments only when operationally supported.
- Record terms acceptance where appropriate.
- Disclose recurring billing, renewal, cancellation, fees, and refund rules clearly.

## Vendor governance
- Inventory processors, subprocessors, AI model providers, analytics providers, embeds, and payment providers.
- Review DPAs, security terms, data retention, training use, breach terms, and international transfer terms.
- Publish a subprocessor list where required or promised.
- Ensure customer logos and testimonials are used with permission.
- Verify image, font, code, and data licenses.

## Legal review
Ask qualified counsel to assess applicable local and international laws. The agent may identify questions and source materials but must not declare legal compliance or produce final legal advice.
