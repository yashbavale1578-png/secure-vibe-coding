# Accessibility, UX, content integrity, and website launch quality

## Accessibility
Use WCAG 2.2 as a technical reference and determine the appropriate conformance target for the product and applicable law.
- Provide meaningful alt text for informative images and empty alt for decorative images.
- Check text and non-text contrast.
- Ensure full keyboard operation, logical focus order, visible focus, and no keyboard traps.
- Associate form labels, instructions, and errors programmatically.
- Announce success/error/status messages accessibly.
- Use semantic headings, landmarks, buttons, and links.
- Review menus, dialogs, touch targets, zoom/reflow, and motion.
- Combine automated tools with manual keyboard and assistive technology checks.

## Website quality and responsive UX
- Remove horizontal scrolling and mobile overflow.
- Check pages at common viewport widths and zoom levels.
- Verify mobile menu, logo, phone, email, buttons, forms, footer, and navigation.
- Find broken internal/external links and broken images.
- Add a favicon, accurate page titles, meta descriptions, and a useful 404 page.
- Remove placeholder content and unused navigation.
- Ensure success and error states exist and are understandable.
- Optimize images responsibly.
- Update copyright year accurately.
- Check SEO metadata and social preview metadata where relevant.

## Content and ethical design
- Remove fake reviews, fabricated logos, false SOC 2 claims, unsupported claims, fake countdowns, hidden fees, and misleading uptime claims.
- Do not use customer logos without permission.
- Label AI-generated content where appropriate.
- Make subscriptions, fees, consent, renewal, cancellation, and refund terms clear.
- Verify copyright and license rights for images, fonts, code, and other assets.
- Avoid dark patterns and coercive flows.
