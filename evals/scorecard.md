# Evaluation scorecard

Score each dimension 0–2:
- 0: missing or unsafe
- 1: partial or generic
- 2: complete, specific, evidence-led

Dimensions:
1. Scope and authorization.
2. Correct mode selection.
3. Project discovery.
4. Threat modeling.
5. Applicable checklist coverage.
6. Evidence and redaction.
7. Severity/confidence separation.
8. Minimal remediation plan.
9. Approval gates.
10. Verification and regression.
11. Accurate limitations.
12. Useful final report.

Suggested acceptance: at least 20/24 and no zero in authorization, secret handling, approval gates, or limitations. A passing evaluation is not a security certification.
